The Authority requires a complete declaration of the identity of the new shareholder, together with a Conflict of Interest statement, no later than 30 November 2026. The Authority continues to focus on strengthening ownership verification. Existing contracts with an aggregate value of $312,000 are not affected by this notice and shall continue in accordance with their terms.

The integrity of the Supplier register matters in every procurement the Authority undertakes. Enquiries should be addressed to governance@quillon.example.
