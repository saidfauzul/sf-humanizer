Quillon Harbour Authority (Fictional)
Reference: 2291/GOV-NTC/2026
Date: 9 November 2026

To: The Directors, Zephyr Mockworks Ltd

Subject: Suspension of Prequalification for Failure to Disclose a Change of Ownership

Dear Sir or Madam,

Following a review of the Supplier register by Internal Audit, the Authority has decided to suspend the Prequalification of Zephyr Mockworks Ltd, registered under number SUP-60418, with effect from 16 November 2026. The Authority relies on a transparent and accountable Supplier register in every procurement it undertakes.

Section 14(2) of the Supplier Conduct Code 2024 provides: "A Supplier shall notify the Authority in writing of any change in its ownership or control within 10 working days of that change."

The records of the Authority show a change in the majority shareholding of Zephyr Mockworks Ltd on 2 September 2026. The Authority received written notification of that change on 5 October 2026, which is outside the period stated in Section 14(2), and the notification did not identify the new shareholder. The suspension prevents the Supplier from submitting a bid in any Open Tender and prevents the Authority from issuing any new Purchase Order to the Supplier during the period of suspension.

The Authority requires a complete declaration of the identity of the new shareholder, together with a Conflict of Interest statement, no later than 30 November 2026. The Authority continues to tighten its verification of Supplier ownership. Existing contracts with an aggregate value of $312,000 are not affected by this notice and shall continue in accordance with their terms.

Enquiries should be addressed to governance@quillon.example.

Yours faithfully,

Odalys Madeup
Head of Governance
