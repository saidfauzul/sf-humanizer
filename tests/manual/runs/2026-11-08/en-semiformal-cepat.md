**Final text**

To: All Finance and Procurement staff
From: Tavi Madeup, Finance Controller
Subject: New procedure for Supplier bank-detail changes (SOP-6620)

SOP-6620 takes effect on 1 December 2026. It applies to every request to change the bank details of a Supplier held in the Nimbus Payables Sandbox.

We need it because a change request can arrive from anyone claiming to be a Supplier. The steps below must be followed in this order:

1. The Accounts Payable Clerk records the request on Form BK-22 on the day it is received and checks the sender's email address against the Supplier file.
2. The Procurement Analyst telephones the Supplier on the number held in the Supplier file, within 2 working days.
3. The Accounts Payable Clerk updates the bank details in the system only after the Procurement Analyst has signed Form BK-22.
4. The Finance Controller decides on any change affecting payments above $25,000, within 1 working day of receiving Form BK-22.
5. The Internal Audit Lead reviews a sample of 10 completed forms every month.

An email reply can't replace the telephone call. To strengthen Segregation of Duties, the Accounts Payable Clerk and the Procurement Analyst must be different people. A change request that fails the telephone check goes to the Internal Audit Lead the same day.

The form protects every payment we make, and it works because each step is clear and has a named owner.

We'll review how the procedure is working after its first 3 months. Please read SOP-6620 before 27 November 2026, and send any questions to me by then.
