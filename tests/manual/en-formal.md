Quillon Harbour Authority (Fictional)

To: The Directors, Zephyr Mockworks Ltd

Subject: Suspension of Prequalification for Failure to Disclose a Change of Ownership

Dear Sir or Madam,

In today's rapidly evolving governance landscape, the integrity of the Supplier register plays a pivotal role in every procurement undertaken by the Authority. Following the conduct of a review of the Supplier register by Internal Audit, the Authority has made a determination to suspend the Prequalification of Zephyr Mockworks Ltd, registered under number SUP-60418, with effect from 16 November 2026.

Section 14(2) of the Supplier Conduct Code 2024 provides: "A Supplier shall notify the Authority in writing of any change in its ownership or control within 10 working days of that change."

It is important to note that the records of the Authority show a change in the majority shareholding of Zephyr Mockworks Ltd on 2 September 2026. Written notification of that change was received by the Authority on 5 October 2026, which is outside the period stated in Section 14(2). Furthermore, the notification did not identify the new shareholder. Moreover, the suspension not only prevents the Supplier from submitting a bid in any Open Tender but also prevents the Authority from issuing any new Purchase Order to the Supplier during the period of suspension.

Additionally, the Authority requires the provision of a complete declaration of the identity of the new shareholder, together with a Conflict of Interest statement, no later than 30 November 2026. The strengthening of the implementation of ownership verification remains the focus of the Authority. Existing contracts with an aggregate value of $312,000 are not affected by this notice and shall continue in accordance with their terms.

Ultimately, this notice underscores the commitment of the Authority to a robust, transparent and accountable Supplier register. Enquiries should be addressed to governance@quillon.example.

Yours faithfully,
