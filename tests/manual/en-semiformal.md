To: All Finance and Procurement staff
From: Tavi Madeup, Finance Controller
Subject: New procedure for Supplier bank-detail changes (SOP-6620)

In today's fast-paced procurement landscape, leveraging a robust and seamless control over payment data is pivotal for every team. It is worth noting that SOP-6620 takes effect on 1 December 2026 and applies to every request to change the bank details of a Supplier held in the Nimbus Payables Sandbox.

Why do we need this? Because a change request can arrive from anyone claiming to be a Supplier. The steps below must be followed in this order:

1. The Accounts Payable Clerk records the request on Form BK-22 on the day it is received and checks the sender's email address against the Supplier file.
2. The Procurement Analyst telephones the Supplier on the number held in the Supplier file, within 2 working days.
3. The Accounts Payable Clerk updates the bank details in the system only after the Procurement Analyst has signed Form BK-22.
4. The Finance Controller decides on any change affecting payments above $25,000, within 1 working day of receiving Form BK-22.
5. The Internal Audit Lead reviews a sample of 10 completed forms every month.

Moreover, the telephone call can't be replaced by an email reply. Furthermore, the strengthening of the implementation of Segregation of Duties is the reason the Accounts Payable Clerk and the Procurement Analyst must be different people. Additionally, a change request that fails the telephone check is passed to the Internal Audit Lead on the same day.

This isn't just a form. It's a safeguard for every payment we make. Overall, this shows that clear steps and named owners are important, valuable and essential.

We will conduct a review of how the procedure is working after its first 3 months. Please read SOP-6620 before 27 November 2026, and send any questions to me by then.
