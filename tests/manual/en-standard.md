How to Register as a Small Supplier with the City of Larkspur Fictional

In today's evolving procurement landscape, navigating the world of public contracts can feel daunting for a small business. Registration with the City of Larkspur Fictional opens on 4 January 2027 under Public Notice 7702/PUB/2026 and closes on 26 February 2027. This guide helps you delve into the process.

Businesses that register through the Larkspur supplier portal win three times as many public contracts as businesses that do not, and registration is the single most effective step a small firm can take to grow.

Who can apply? Any business with fewer than 50 employees can apply. It is worth noting that the registration fee is $40, and you must make a payment of this fee before the City will conduct an evaluation of your application.

To apply, follow these steps:

1. Create a business profile on the portal.
2. Upload your trade license and your most recent tax certificate.
3. Pay the fee of $40 by card.
4. Submit the application and keep the reference number that you receive.

Moreover, the City will carry out a review of your application within 15 working days. Furthermore, the City not only checks your documents but also confirms that your trade license is still valid. Additionally, if something is missing, the City will send you an email and you will have 10 working days to provide the missing document.

Registration is valid for 2 years. It isn't a contract. It's a robust and seamless pathway to opportunity. Once you are registered, you can respond to any Request for Quotation that the City publishes for small businesses.

Overall, this shows that the process is truly straightforward. To begin, visit https://larkspur.example/suppliers, or email suppliers@larkspur.example if you have a question.
