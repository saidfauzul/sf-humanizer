## Before

To: All teams
From: Procurement
Subject: Changes to the Purchase Request Process

From 1 December 2026, purchase requests under $1,000 need only a team lead's approval. This change is intended to speed up the approval of low-value purchases, which currently spend more time in the queue than the purchases themselves justify. Requests of $1,000 or more will continue to follow the existing approval route.

The basic steps for a request remain the same. Under the updated process, a request moves through three numbered steps:

1. The team completes form PR-01 with the item, quantity, estimated cost, and justification.
2. The team lead approves the request within 2 working days.
3. The Procurement Officer processes the request.

Please check that form PR-01 is complete before submitting it. Requests with missing information will be returned, and this will add delay for everyone. Team leads should make sure that approvals are given within the two working day window, or let the Procurement Officer know if there is a reason for delay.

We will review the new process after the first three months to see whether it is working as intended. Feedback is welcome at any time, and can be sent to the Procurement Officer or to your team lead.

Thank you for your cooperation in making this change work well.
