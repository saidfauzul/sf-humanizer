## Before

Example Transit Agency
Procurement Notice

Reference: RFP-2026-09
Open Tender for Security Services

1. Introduction
The Example Transit Agency invites the submission of bids by qualified security services providers for the provision of security services at its premises under Open Tender reference RFP-2026-09. This notice sets out the key requirements and deadlines for participation. Interested suppliers should conduct a review of the full tender documents before preparing their submissions.

2. Submission Deadline
It should be noted that bids must be submitted no later than 4 December 2026 at 2:00 p.m. Bids received after this time will not be considered. Late submissions will be returned unopened.

3. Bid Security
Moreover, each bid must be accompanied by a Bid Bond of $5,000. Furthermore, the Bid Bond must be issued by a financial institution acceptable to the Example Transit Agency and must remain valid for the period specified in the tender documents.

4. Clarification Questions
Suppliers may submit questions about the tender until 25 November 2026. Questions should be sent in writing to the Procurement Unit. Responses to all questions will be shared with all registered suppliers, without identifying the party that asked, so that all bidders have the same information.

5. Eligibility
Bidders must meet the eligibility criteria stated in the tender documents, including relevant licensing and experience requirements for security services.

6. Further Information
The Example Transit Agency reserves the right to amend the tender, or to cancel the procurement, in accordance with applicable rules.

Issued by the Procurement Unit, Example Transit Agency.
