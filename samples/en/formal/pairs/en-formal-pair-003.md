## Before

Sample Agency
Office of the Contracting Officer

Reference: CO-2026-114

To: Fictional Supplies Inc.

Subject: Notice to Lodge Performance Bond

Dear Sir or Madam,

Further to the Letter of Award issued to Fictional Supplies Inc. under this reference, the Sample Agency requires the lodgement of a Performance Bond in the amount of $12,500. Under the terms of the award, this bond must be received by the Sample Agency no later than 20 November 2026.

Please arrange for the Performance Bond to be issued by a financial institution acceptable to the Sample Agency, and lodge the original document with the Contracting Officer's office. The bond must be valid for the full period of the contract, and its terms must be consistent with the conditions set out in the tender documents.

If the Performance Bond is not lodged by 20 November 2026, the Sample Agency may withdraw the Letter of Award without further notice. In that event, the Sample Agency reserves the right to proceed with the procurement in any manner it considers appropriate, and no contract will be formed on the basis of the withdrawn award.

If you have any questions about the bond requirement or the lodgement process, please contact this office in writing quoting reference CO-2026-114. We will respond as promptly as practicable.

Thank you for your cooperation in this matter. We look forward to receiving the Performance Bond within the stated timeframe.

Yours faithfully,

Contracting Officer
Sample Agency
