## Before

FORMAL POLICY NOTICE

Declaration of Conflicts of Interest for Procurement Staff

Purpose

It is important to note that this notice sets out a mandatory requirement for the making of a declaration of any Conflict of Interest by all staff involved in procurement activities. The requirement is intended not only to protect the integrity of the procurement process but also to safeguard the organization's reputation and to ensure that decisions are made fairly and impartially.

Scope

This requirement applies to every member of staff who takes part in any stage of a procurement process, including the preparation of requirements, the evaluation of bids, the negotiation of contracts, and the management of suppliers.

Requirement

Furthermore, any staff member who identifies a Conflict of Interest must make a declaration of it in writing. Moreover, declarations must be submitted to Internal Audit within 3 working days of the conflict becoming known to the staff member.

Effective Date

This requirement takes effect on 1 December 2026.

Consequences of Non-Compliance

Failure to declare a Conflict of Interest may result in disciplinary action in accordance with the applicable policies of the organization. Staff members who are uncertain whether a particular interest constitutes a conflict are encouraged to seek guidance from Internal Audit before taking any further action.

Enquiries

Questions about this notice should be directed to Internal Audit.
