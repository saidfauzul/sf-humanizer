# Formal (English)

> Draft register definition.

Official and institutional prose: policy, regulation, contracts, formal reports. Complete sentences, no contractions, precise terminology, and citations kept exactly as written.

Features of this register:

- Complete sentences and no contractions.
- Precise terminology, with defined terms used consistently.
- Citations, reference numbers and clause numbers kept exactly as written.
- Passive voice where regulations and contracts normally use it.

Text types covered:

- Policy summaries and policy notices
- Official letters
- Procurement notices and formal reports

- `good/`: examples of good human writing.
- `pairs/`: before-and-after rewrite pairs.

The sample format is described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md).
