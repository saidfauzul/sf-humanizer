Sample Island Ferry Authority
Procurement Notice

Reference: RFQ-2026-41
Request for Quotation: Replacement Passenger Seating, MV Example Gull

1. Requirement
The Authority seeks quotations for the removal of 212 passenger seats from the upper deck of MV Example Gull and the supply and fitting of 196 replacement seats. The reduction of 16 seats is deliberate. It widens the aisle to the 900 mm required by the Fictional Maritime Code, Part 8.

2. Timing
The vessel will be out of service at Sampleton Slipway from 11 January 2027 to 29 January 2027, and it must return to the Monday timetable on 1 February 2027. Work cannot begin earlier, since the vessel carries school traffic until the end of term.

3. Seats
Each seat must carry a current fire certificate to Standard FM-114. Quotations that offer a seat without that certificate will be rejected.

4. Site Visit
One visit will be held on 2 December 2026 at 09:30, on board the vessel at Pier 3. Attendance is optional. Measurements taken by the Authority in 2024 are in Annex B and should be checked, because the deck was resurfaced in 2025.

5. Quotations
Quotations must state a fixed price in dollars, valid for 90 days, and must reach the Procurement Unit by 16:00 on 10 December 2026. The Authority has budgeted no more than $148,000 and will not consider a higher price.
