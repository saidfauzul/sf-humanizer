Summary of Policy GP-07/2026: Gifts and Hospitality from Suppliers

Sample Port Authority, Compliance Office

The Board adopted Policy GP-07/2026 on 14 September 2026, after the 2025 internal review found 37 supplier gifts that appeared in no register. The policy replaces GP-03/2019 from 1 January 2027. This summary does not alter its terms, and where the two differ, the policy prevails.

Staff who take part in a tender may accept nothing from any bidder from the date the tender is advertised until 30 days after the contract is signed. Outside those periods, a member of staff may accept a gift worth up to $25, provided that the supplier has given that person no other gift in the same calendar year. Cash and vouchers must be refused at any value.

Every gift offered must be entered in the Gifts Register within 5 working days, including a gift that was refused. The Compliance Office reads the Register each quarter and reports to the Audit Committee any supplier named more than three times.

Hospitality is treated differently, because a meal at a supplier's site is sometimes unavoidable. A meal during an inspection visit is permitted if the visit lasts more than four hours and the nearest public restaurant is over 10 kilometres away. The officer must record the meal in the Register on returning to the office.

A breach of the policy may lead to disciplinary action under Staff Regulation 22. Questions should be addressed to Mara Example, Compliance Officer.
