Sample Water Board
Office of the Chief Procurement Officer

Reference: SWB/CPO/2026/318
12 October 2026

To: The Director, Example Pipeworks Ltd

Subject: Revised Delivery Programme under Contract SWB-C-2041

Dear Director,

Your letter of 6 October 2026 reports that the foundry at Fictionville cannot cast the 400 mm valves before February 2027, because its only furnace failed on 28 September 2026. The Board accepts that account. It has read the enclosed engineer's report and has no reason to doubt it.

The Board cannot, however, accept the revised programme attached to your letter. Contract SWB-C-2041 requires all 18 valves to be installed at the Example Reservoir outlet before spring filling begins on 1 April 2027. Your programme delivers the last six valves on 22 March 2027 and leaves nine days for installation. The Board's engineers need at least 21.

Two courses are open under Clause 14.3 of the Contract. Example Pipeworks Ltd may subcontract the casting of the six valves, at its own cost, to a foundry on the Board's approved list. Alternatively, it may deliver twelve valves by the original date, and the Board will postpone filling of the east basin only. In that case the Contract Price will be reduced by $46,000 to cover the additional pumping.

Please state your choice in writing by 26 October 2026. If the Board has received no reply by that date, it will treat the delay as a default under Clause 19.1.

Yours faithfully,

Tomas Sample
Chief Procurement Officer
