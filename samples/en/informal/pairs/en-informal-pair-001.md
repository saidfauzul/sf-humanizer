## Before

Hey,

I hope this message finds you well! Did you survive Monday's committee meeting? I'm still recovering. It was supposed to wrap up in the afternoon, but it ran until 7 p.m. because the Technical Specifications changed three times. Every time we thought we were finally done, someone found something else to tweak, and by the end we were all just staring at each other, too tired to even complain properly.

Honestly, it wasn't a meeting, it was an endurance event. I almost walked out halfway through, but it felt rude to leave in the middle of it all, so I stuck it out. When it finally broke up I felt like I'd run a marathon. Thankfully there was still some instant coffee in the pantry, which truly saved what was left of the evening.

Anyway, I was thinking we could take a proper break this week. Why lunch on Wednesday in the cafeteria? Because it'd be nice to sit down, eat something, relax a bit, and talk about literally anything other than specs and revisions. Moreover, I've also been wanting to try the new dishes at the corner stall. Furthermore, I hear they're pretty good.

Let me know if Wednesday works for you. If it doesn't, we can easily find another day. I'm just really looking forward to some time away from the committee and our endless deadlines.

Hope your week's going a little smoother than mine.

Talk soon, and see you at the cafeteria!
