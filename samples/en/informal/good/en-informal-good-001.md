Forty boxes and one wet Tuesday

So the basement flooded. Only about an inch, but that was enough to soak the bottom row of the archive shelves, which is where somebody in 2011 decided to keep the old contract files. Forty boxes. I know because I carried every one of them up the stairs, since the lift picked that morning to break too.

Half of them were fine. The other half smelled like a pond.

I spent the rest of the week with Tomasz Example from Facilities, peeling damp Purchase Orders apart and laying them out on the training room floor. We ran out of floor by Wednesday and moved on to the corridor. People stepped over us with their coffee and said things like "wow" and "good luck." Nobody offered to help, and I can't say I blame them.

Here's the thing I didn't see coming. About a third of what we dried out had already been scanned years ago. Nobody knew. There's a spreadsheet, last touched in 2019, sitting in a folder called "misc," and it lists every box by number. Tomasz found it Friday at about 4 p.m. I had to go for a walk.

Anyway. The shelves go up on blocks next month. And I've put that spreadsheet somewhere people can actually find it, with my name on it, so at least the next person knows who to shout at.
