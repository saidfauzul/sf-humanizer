# Informal (English)

> Draft register definition.

Everyday writing: personal posts, notes to friends or close colleagues. Contractions, fragments and a conversational rhythm are all acceptable.

Features of the register:

- Contractions and sentence fragments are normal.
- A personal voice, with specific small details and asides.
- Uneven rhythm that follows how the writer would say it.
- Greetings, jokes and sign-offs stay when they suit the relationship.

Text types covered: blog-style posts, team newsletter items, social posts and notes to friends or close colleagues.

- `good/`: examples of good human writing.
- `pairs/`: before-and-after rewrite pairs.
