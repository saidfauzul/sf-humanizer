# Chat (English)

> Draft register definition.

Instant messaging such as Slack or WhatsApp: short lines, direct, minimal punctuation and no letter-style greetings or sign-offs.

Features of the register:

- Turns are short and uneven, and fragments are fine when the thread supplies the context.
- Each turn in a thread starts with the speaker's name in bold, for example **Maya:**.
- Numbers, dates, names and locked terms stay exact even when the tone is casual.

Text types: Slack or Teams threads with a vendor contact, with a manager, and with a teammate.

- `good/`: examples of good human writing.
- `pairs/`: before-and-after rewrite pairs.
