## Before

Frequently Asked Questions: Registering as a Vendor with the Sample Agency

Who can register as a Vendor?
Any small business that wants to supply goods or services to the Sample Agency can apply to register as a Vendor. Registration is open to businesses of all types that meet the basic requirements described below.

Does it cost anything to register?
No. Registration is free. You will not be asked to pay any fee to submit an application, and you should be cautious of any third party that claims otherwise.

What do I need to register?
A valid business number is required for every application. Your business number will be checked as part of the registration process, so please make sure it is accurate and up to date before you apply.

How long does registration take?
Registration can take up to 10 working days. The time needed depends on how complete your application is and how quickly any follow-up questions are answered. Applications with missing information may take longer, so please check your details carefully before you submit.

What happens after I register?
Once your registration is approved, your business will be listed in the Sample Agency's vendor records. Being registered does not guarantee that you will receive any contract. It does mean that your business can be considered when opportunities for which you are eligible are published.

Where can I get help?
If you have questions about the registration process, please contact the Sample Agency through the channels listed on this website. We aim to answer enquiries as quickly as we can.
