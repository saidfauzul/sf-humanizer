Getting paid by Example Town Council

Email your invoice to invoices@example.test and put your Purchase Order number in the subject line. We pay within 21 days of receiving an invoice we can match to a Purchase Order. If the number is missing, our system sends the invoice back to you and the 21 days don't start.

Your Purchase Order number is the letters EX followed by six digits, such as EX-104233. It's at the top of the order we emailed you. If you can't find it, ask the person who placed the order. Our accounts team can't look it up, because orders are filed by department and they can't see who your contact was.

Before you send your invoice, check that it shows:

1. your business name and address, spelled as they are on the order
2. the Purchase Order number
3. the date you delivered the goods or finished the work
4. the amount, with tax shown on its own line

If you deliver an order in stages, send one invoice for each delivery. We can't pay a single invoice against two delivery dates.

Most late payments we see come from a changed bank account. If yours has changed, write to us at least 10 days before your next invoice, and phone your contact as well. We ask for both because fraudsters sometimes send fake change requests by email.
