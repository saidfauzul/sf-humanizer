School meals contract: help for first-time Bidders

This page answers the questions Example County's Bidders ask most often about tender EC-2027-018, the contract to supply lunches to 14 primary schools from September 2027.

Closing date
Your bid must reach us by 4 p.m. on 26 February 2027. The portal shuts at that minute, and we can't accept a bid that's half uploaded. Give yourself an hour.

If you've never run a school kitchen
You can still bid. You'll need to show that you've cooked for at least 200 people a day for a year or more, in any setting. Hospitals, care homes and factory canteens all count.

Bidding for some of the schools
You can bid for one group or both. The north group has nine schools and the south group has five, and the map in the tender pack shows which is which. We won't accept a bid for individual schools.

Asking a question
Send questions through the portal by 5 February 2027. We publish every question and answer for all Bidders to read, with your name removed. Please don't phone the schools. Head teachers have been told to refer you back to us, and a call may count against your bid.

The tasting day
Shortlisted Bidders cook one meal for a panel of six, including two pupils, on 18 March 2027 at Example Primary. We pay for your ingredients up to 150 pounds. Keep the receipt.
